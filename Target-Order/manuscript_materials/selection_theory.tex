\subsubsection{A Validation-Based Order-Selection Guarantee}
\label{subsubsec:validated-order}
The pilot-quantile score is a heuristic. A finite-candidate procedure with
an explicit excess-risk guarantee is available by using held-out log loss.
It applies equally to graphs constructed by FGCR, ROS, or an exact
ordered-independence oracle.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-Candidate Validation Oracle Inequality]
\label{thm:validation-selection}
Condition on all order generation, pilot data, and fitting data.
Let $\widehat Q_1,\ldots,\widehat Q_K$ be the resulting strictly positive
joint estimates on $R$, and let $Z_1,\ldots,Z_m$ be independent of these
estimates and i.i.d.\ from $P^C$. Suppose
$0\leq-\log\widehat Q_k(z)\leq B$ for all $k,z$, with a finite
$B$ determined before validation. Set
\[
\widehat k\in\argmin_{1\leq k\leq K}
\frac1m\sum_{i=1}^m-\log\widehat Q_k(Z_i).
\]
With conditional probability at least $1-\delta$,
\begin{equation}
D_{\rm KL}(P^C\Vert\widehat Q_{\widehat k})
\leq \min_{1\leq k\leq K}D_{\rm KL}(P^C\Vert\widehat Q_k)
 +2B\sqrt{\frac{\log(2K/\delta)}{2m}}.
\label{eq:selection-oracle}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Conditional on the fitted candidates, Hoeffding's inequality and a union
bound give $|\widehat L_k-L_k|\leq\eta$ simultaneously for all $k$,
where $\eta=B\sqrt{\log(2K/\delta)/(2m)}$ and
$L_k=\mathbb E_{P^C}[-\log\widehat Q_k(Z)]$.
If $k^\star$ minimizes $L_k$, then
$L_{\widehat k}\leq\widehat L_{\widehat k}+\eta
\leq\widehat L_{k^\star}+\eta\leq L_{k^\star}+2\eta$.
Subtract the common entropy $H(P^C)$ to obtain the KL inequality.
\end{proof}

For BDeu fits a computable choice is
\[
B=\max_k\sum_{v\in R}
\log\frac{1}{\min_{a,x}\widehat p_{k,v}(x\mid a)}.
\]
Strictly positive pseudocounts make this finite even with empty cells.
The guarantee compares the realized fitted candidates, not an unknown
globally optimal topological order; it does not eliminate the fitting
error of any candidate. Its dependence on $B$ can also be conservative.
The selected estimate must not be refitted on the validation observations
and then assigned the same certificate without further analysis.
Duplicates may be removed before validation: only distinct fitted
distributions need enter $K$.
